Download Excel

One click exports every active customer into a single sheet — CRM rows plus Follow-up (Contacts, Follow, and Meeting), using Status and dates.

  • Status: NONE stays in CRM; DONE moves permanently into Follow-up.
  • Meeting date → Meeting · Follow-up date → Follow · Done with no dates → Contacts.
  • All purchaser and maintenance contacts are included (Primary through Seventh — up to 7 each).

Upload Excel

Import a .xlsx / .xls backup. It becomes the active dataset and appears in CRM and Follow-up according to each row’s Status and dates.

  • Required column: Industry Name.
  • Optional: Status, Follow-up Entered, dates, and Purchaser / Maintenance 1–7 Name, Phone, Email columns (Primary is used in list views).
  • This replaces the current active sheet (previous uploads stay in History).
